FAQ

Ask us directly
01What is AI business process automation?

It means using AI and automation tools to handle repetitive work: answering customers, creating orders, data entry, reconciliation, reminders and reports. Your staff only handle what needs a human decision, so the business runs faster with fewer errors.

02Does a small business need automation?

Yes, and small businesses often gain the most. The Starter package, from 5,000,000 VND (≈ US$200), automates your most time-consuming workflow, such as taking in and qualifying customers from Zalo, and goes live in 5 to 7 days.

03Which software can the automation connect to?

Zalo OA, Facebook Messenger, your website, Google Sheets, Gmail, Google Drive, and any CRM, sales or accounting software with an API. Dream Labs uses n8n, Make or custom code depending on scale and security needs.

04Is our business data safe?

Your data stays in your own accounts and systems. The Enterprise package can run on a private server with role-based access, and internal data never leaves unless it has to.

05How long does a website take?

Essential takes 7–10 days, Professional 14–21 days and Premium 30–45 days, depending on pages and features. A detailed schedule is agreed right after the discovery call.

06How does payment work?

50% deposit on signing, 50% on acceptance. Projects over 30,000,000 VND are paid in three instalments: 40 / 30 / 30.

07How many revisions do I get?

Each package states its number of revision rounds. Technical fixes during the warranty period are always free and don’t count towards them.

08Does Dream Labs write copy and shoot photos?

Yes. Professional packages and above include SEO copywriting. Product photography and filming are quoted separately per session.

09Are the projects on this page for real clients?

Not yet. Brands labeled Concept are projects Dream Labs imagined and built itself so you can see the craft first-hand: working websites, real video and AI demos you can try.
